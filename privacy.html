隱私權政策

《落霞一生》不收集、不儲存、也不傳送任何個人資料。

  • 本遊戲沒有帳號、伺服器、廣告、分析或追蹤工具。
  • 遊戲進度與設定只保存在您的裝置上，不會傳送給開發者或任何第三方。刪除本遊戲時，這些資料也會一併刪除。若您以 iCloud 或電腦備份整台裝置，備份中可能包含這些資料，由您自己保管。
  • 本遊戲可離線遊玩，不會與開發者通訊。1.0 版完全不使用網路連線。
  • 遊戲由 App Store（iPhone）或 Google Play（Android）販售，付款由 Apple 或 Google 處理，開發者無法取得您的付款資訊。Apple 與 Google 如何處理資料，請參閱各自的隱私權政策。
  • 本遊戲不蒐集任何人的資料，包括兒童。
  • 本政策若有變更，將在此頁面公告並更新日期。

Privacy Policy

落霞一生 does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • The game has no accounts, servers, ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer or any third party. Deleting the game deletes this data. If you back up your whole device with iCloud or a computer, that backup may include this data, and it stays under your control.
  • The game can be played offline and does not communicate with the developer. Version 1.0 makes no network connections at all.
  • The game is sold through the App Store (iPhone) and Google Play (Android). Payment is handled by Apple or Google; the developer never receives your payment information. See Apple's and Google's privacy policies for how they handle data.
  • The game collects no data from anyone, including children.
  • Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
